# Sarah's Brief — NorthWave Analytical Foundation

**From:** Sarah Mitchell, VP of Analytics, NorthWave
**Subject:** Building an analytical foundation

NorthWave has grown across five countries — Germany, UK, USA, Italy, and France — but our reporting is a patchwork of spreadsheets and disconnected tools. I need an analytical foundation that answers business questions consistently, fast, and without weeks of manual work each time.

Here are the five questions I need our data to answer reliably:

1. **Customer profitability by tier** — which customer tiers (Platinum, Premium, Standard) drive the most revenue and gross margin? Where should we invest in retention versus acquisition?

2. **Marketing ROI by channel** — for each marketing event we run, what's the resulting revenue from targeted customers? Which channels actually move the needle?

3. **Inventory turns by region** — how are our warehouses performing? Are we over-stocking in some regions while running out in others?

4. **Year-over-year sales growth** — broken down by product category and country. Where are we growing, where are we shrinking?

5. **Customer retention by signup cohort** — of customers who signed up in a given quarter, how many are still active a year later? Two years later? Are we retaining the customers we acquire?

I need this within six weeks. Budget for tooling is minimal — we should be able to build this with modern, mostly-free tools. AI assistance is encouraged.
